# Original assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then copied into this repository. No CLI/API fallback was used. All three outputs were visually inspected by the image agents.

- `public/art/world.png`: 1536×1024. Prompt: cinematic painterly realistic ancient myth-inspired Iran; rugged layered mountains, dry ochre valley, distant Persian stone fortress and caravanserai; lone warrior in scale armor and crimson scarf on far left; amber late-afternoon light; dark olive and charcoal shadows; environment dominant, crop-friendly panorama, no text/logo/UI, original artwork.
- `public/art/enemies.png`: 1536×1024. Prompt: one landscape atlas with three exactly equal vertical thirds, edge to edge: dark gray wolf in Iranian foothills; masked ancient Persian brigand in weathered brown clothing with dagger; outlaw captain in bronze scale armor and crimson turban. Centered upper-body portraits, shared painterly realism, charcoal/olive shadows and antique-gold highlights; no borders, text, logos or UI. CSS positions this atlas for individual cards.
- `public/og.png`: 1536×1024. Prompt: premium cinematic Persian RPG title card, myth-inspired Iranian mountain fortress, small warrior with crimson scarf left; exact large Persian title «مبارز» and supporting copy «افسانه‌ات را زندگی کن» right; charcoal/dark olive/antique gold/amber; readable joined RTL typography, no other text, watermark or logos. The output includes decorative winged motifs and slight visual spacing around the possessive suffix.
- `public/art/character/*`: production character sprites sliced from Codex-generated transparent atlases on 14 September 2026. Female and male atlases each contain six aligned full-body looks (base underclothing, leather, scale, guardian, travel costume, ceremonial costume). A separate wearable atlas supplies the iron, bronze and sun blades plus cypress and Simorgh charms. RGB cleanup attempts that painted a checkerboard were discarded in favor of the earlier genuine-alpha outputs; small fringe speckles were reduced before slicing. These are still complete outfit variants plus overlays, not independently authored layer PSDs.

Typography: Vazirmatn, self-hosted static weights 400/600/800 from Google Fonts. License: SIL Open Font License; included in `public/fonts/OFL.txt`.
